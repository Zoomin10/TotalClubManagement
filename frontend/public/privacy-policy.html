Privacy Policy

Wroughton & Wichelstowe Football Club
Last updated: April 2026

This privacy policy explains how Wroughton & Wichelstowe Football Club collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information submitted through the digital player registration and consent form.

1. Who We Are

Wroughton & Wichelstowe Football Club (“WWFC”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is a football club based in the United Kingdom. We collect and process player registration and consent information so that we can safely and effectively manage club participation.

2. What Information We Collect

When a parent, guardian, or carer completes the digital consent form, we may collect:

  • Player name, date of birth, gender, and age group
  • Parent, guardian, or emergency contact details
  • Phone numbers and email addresses
  • Postcode and house number
  • Relationship to the player
  • Important medical information, including medication details
  • Allergy information, including food intolerances
  • Consent preferences for contact, photographs, and videos
  • Parent/carer printed name, signature, and signature date

3. Why We Use This Information

We use this information to:

  • Register players with the club
  • Contact parents, guardians, or carers about club matters
  • Support player welfare, safeguarding, and emergency care
  • Record consent preferences for photography, video, and communication
  • Manage club administration and player records

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under UK data protection law, we rely on appropriate lawful bases for processing personal data, including:

  • Consent — where you give permission for specific uses, such as storing contact details or using photos/videos.
  • Legitimate interests — for running the club safely and effectively.
  • Legal obligation — where information may be required for safeguarding or other legal responsibilities.
  • Vital interests — where medical or emergency contact information may be needed to protect a player in an emergency.

5. Medical and Safeguarding Information

Medical and allergy information is collected so that coaches, volunteers, or authorised club representatives can respond appropriately where a player may need support or emergency assistance. This information should only be accessed by those who need it for player welfare, safeguarding, or emergency purposes.

6. Photos, Videos, and Social Media

The form asks for separate consent for photographs and videos. These may be used on WWFC websites, social media channels, and WWFC-endorsed promotional materials only where consent has been given.

WWFC aims to follow relevant England FA guidance on safeguarding, photography, video, and social media use.

7. How We Store and Protect Data

Registration data is stored securely using cloud-hosted systems and a PostgreSQL database. Access to registration data should be limited to authorised club administrators. We take reasonable steps to protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

8. How Long We Keep Data

We will keep player registration and consent information only for as long as needed for club administration, safeguarding, insurance, and legal purposes. Records should be reviewed periodically and securely deleted when no longer required.

9. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell personal data. We may share information only where necessary, such as with:

  • Club coaches, volunteers, or administrators who need access for club purposes
  • Safeguarding authorities, where required
  • Emergency services, where needed
  • Football governing bodies, where required for club administration or compliance
  • Technology providers used to host and operate the system

10. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete information where appropriate
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Object to certain types of processing
  • Raise a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)
